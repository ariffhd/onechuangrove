The Developer Consortium

Sing Holdings · Sunway Developments

One Chuan Grove is jointly developed by Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. — a joint venture that committed $1.33 billion across two Government Land Sales tenders to secure and amalgamate the Lorong Chuan site.

The Consortium

Two Developers. One Landmark Project.

Sing Holdings Residential and Sunway Developments partnered to win not one but two adjoining Government Land Sales tenders at Lorong Chuan in 2025 — a back-to-back result that speaks to their shared conviction in the site. Together they represent decades of experience, listed-company financial discipline, and a proven track record of delivering residential projects across Singapore.

The joint venture develops One Chuan Grove through a dedicated project vehicle, Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd. (URA Housing Developer's Licence C1558) — held 65% by Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. and 35% by Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd.

About Sing Holdings

Sing Holdings Limited is a Singapore property development and investment group founded in 1964 and listed on the SGX Mainboard since 2007. Its largest shareholder is F. H. Lee Holdings (Pte) Limited, with approximately 36% of shares outstanding; Sze Hao Lee has served as Chief Executive Officer since 2009, having joined the Group as an Executive Director in 1992.

Sing Holdings' residential portfolio includes North Gaia, an executive condominium in Yishun; Parc Botannia in Sengkang, developed with Wee Hur Holdings; Robin Residences at Robin Drive; Waterwoods in Punggol; and The Laurels at Cairnhill Road, winner of the FIABCI Singapore Property Award 2015 in the Residential (Mid-Rise) category.

Role in the Chuan Grove GLS Tenders

Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. holds the majority 65% stake in the joint venture behind One Chuan Grove, having partnered with Sunway Developments to win both Government Land Sales tenders at Lorong Chuan in 2025 — the first on 8 July ($703.6M, $1,376 psf ppr) and the second on 4 September ($623.9M, $1,331 psf ppr).

About Sunway Developments

Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. is a wholly-owned Singapore subsidiary of Sunway Berhad, the Malaysian conglomerate founded in 1974 by Tan Sri Dr Jeffrey Cheah and listed on Bursa Malaysia. Sunway's Singapore residential push began in 2021, when a Sunway-led consortium with Hoi Hup Realty won a Government Land Sale tender for $371 million.

In September 2025, Sunway Berhad announced the RM2.3 billion acquisition of MCL Land — its largest deal to date — rebranding its Singapore residential arm as Sunway MCL. The enlarged Singapore business carries forward MCL Land's existing portfolio, including Tembusu Grand, The Continuum and Parc Esta.

Role in the Chuan Grove GLS Tenders

Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. holds a 35% stake in the joint venture behind One Chuan Grove, contributing to both winning Government Land Sales bids at Lorong Chuan in 2025 alongside Sing Holdings Residential.

Why It Matters

What the Consortium Means for Buyers

A listed Singapore developer and the Singapore arm of a listed Malaysian conglomerate committing $1.33 billion to win two adjoining Government Land Sales tenders is not a routine transaction. It reflects real conviction in the Lorong Chuan site — and it carries direct implications for buyers considering One Chuan Grove.

01
Financial Certainty
Sing Holdings Limited is SGX Mainboard-listed with full disclosure obligations; Sunway Berhad is listed on Bursa Malaysia under equivalent regulatory oversight. Both developers in the JV are accountable to public shareholders — construction timeline risk is standard for any new launch purchase, but developer solvency risk is low.
02
Quality Assurance
Both Sing Holdings and Sunway have public reputations to protect across their respective markets. Buyers can expect construction quality and delivery standards consistent with each developer's existing portfolio — see their track records above.
03
Market Conviction
A blended land price of $1,331–$1,376 psf ppr across two tenders — won 7.3% and 2.9% clear of the next-highest bids respectively — reflects real conviction in the Lorong Chuan location, not a cautious or opportunistic bid.
