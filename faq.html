About One Chuan Grove

About One Chuan Grove

Q1.
What is One Chuan Grove?

One Chuan Grove is a forthcoming mixed residential and commercial development at Lorong Chuan, District 19, Singapore. It comprises 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space, per URA Housing Developer's Licence C1558, and is developed by a joint venture between Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd.

Q2.
Where is One Chuan Grove located?

One Chuan Grove is located at Lorong Chuan, District 19, Singapore, near Lorong Chuan MRT Station (CC14, Circle Line). The development's address is 1 Chuan Grove, Singapore 556860.

Q3.
Who is developing One Chuan Grove?

One Chuan Grove is developed by a joint venture between Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. (65%) and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. (35%), through their project company Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd. (URA Housing Developer's Licence C1558). Sing Holdings Residential is a subsidiary of SGX Mainboard-listed Sing Holdings Limited; Sunway Developments is a subsidiary of Bursa Malaysia-listed Sunway Berhad.

Q4.
How many units will One Chuan Grove have?

One Chuan Grove is licensed for 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space, per URA Housing Developer's Licence C1558. The unit mix and tower configuration will be announced closer to launch.

Q5.
How big is the site?

The site comprises two adjoining Government Land Sales parcels of 170,409 sq ft and 156,231 sq ft, approximately 326,640 sq ft in total.

Q6.
How was the site acquired?

Through two Government Land Sales tenders. The first parcel closed on 8 July 2025 and the adjoining second parcel on 4 September 2025; both were awarded to the Sing Holdings-Sunway Developments joint venture.

Location & Connectivity

Location & Connectivity

Q13.
Is One Chuan Grove near an MRT station?

Yes. One Chuan Grove is near Lorong Chuan MRT Station (CC14) on the Circle Line, one stop from Bishan (North-South Line interchange) and one stop from Serangoon (North East Line interchange). Walking time will be confirmed closer to launch.

Q14.
What schools are near One Chuan Grove?

St. Gabriel's Primary School (220 Lorong Chuan) and the Australian International School (1 Lorong Chuan) are on Lorong Chuan, with other schools in the wider Serangoon area. Distances will be confirmed closer to launch; please check MOE's official school distance checker for primary school registration.

Q15.
What shopping and amenities are nearby?

NEX shopping mall, Serangoon Public Library and the Serangoon Bus Interchange are one MRT stop away at Serangoon. Neighbourhood parks and the Serangoon Garden food scene are also nearby.

Buying & Eligibility

Buying & Eligibility

Q16.
Can HDB owners buy One Chuan Grove?

Yes. Singapore Citizens who own an HDB flat can purchase One Chuan Grove as their first private property and pay 0% ABSD, provided they sell their HDB flat within 6 months of One Chuan Grove's TOP or purchase completion, whichever is earlier. This is commonly referred to as the HDB upgrader pathway.

Q17.
Can foreigners buy One Chuan Grove?

Yes, foreigners are eligible to purchase One Chuan Grove, a private residential unit, subject to Singapore's Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) rules. Foreign buyers currently pay 60% ABSD on residential property purchases.
