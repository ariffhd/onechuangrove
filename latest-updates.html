URA Issues Housing Developer's Licence C1558

The Urban Redevelopment Authority issued Housing Developer's Licence C1558 to Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd., the project vehicle of the Sing Holdings Residential–Sunway Developments joint venture, authorising development of 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space across Lots 19037L and 19064A MK18 at Chuan Grove.

The licence confirms United Overseas Bank Limited as the project account bank and Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd.'s registered address as 96 Robinson Road, #10-01 SIF Building, Singapore 068899. This is the clearest official confirmation yet of the development's scale and structure.

Facility plans, unit mix breakdown, floor plans and launch timing have not yet been announced. Register to be notified as these are confirmed.

Second Government Land Sales Parcel Awarded

The tender for the second, adjoining Government Land Sales parcel (156,231 sq ft) closed on 4 September 2025 and was awarded to the Sing Holdings Residential–Sunway Developments joint venture.

With both parcels secured, the two sites can be developed as a single development of approximately 326,640 sq ft.

Site Background

About the Site — Two Parcels, One Development

One Chuan Grove is being developed on two adjoining Government Land Sales parcels at Lorong Chuan.

8 Jul 2025
First GLS Parcel Awarded
The first parcel (170,409 sq ft) was awarded to the Sing Holdings Residential–Sunway Developments joint venture.
4 Sep 2025
Second GLS Parcel Awarded
The adjoining parcel (156,231 sq ft) was awarded to the same joint venture, allowing the two parcels to be developed as a single ~326,640 sq ft site.
31 Jul 2026
URA Developer's Licence C1558 Issued
URA licensed Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd. — the joint venture's project company — to develop 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space across the site.
