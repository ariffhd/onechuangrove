Amenities · Serangoon

NEX & Serangoon Lifestyle Guide

One stop from Lorong Chuan MRT sits Serangoon — home to NEX mall, the Serangoon Bus Interchange, and Serangoon Public Library. Closer still, a couple of neighbourhood parks sit within a few hundred metres.

Shopping & Transport Hub

NEX — One Stop from Lorong Chuan MRT

NEX is one of the largest shopping malls in the north-east of Singapore, sitting directly above Serangoon MRT/Bus Interchange — one Circle Line stop from Lorong Chuan.

What's at NEX

A Full-Scale Regional Mall

NEX houses a supermarket, a cinema, and the Serangoon Public Library, alongside a wide mix of retail, dining and services spread across multiple floors — the kind of one-stop regional mall that's genuinely useful for daily errands, not just occasional visits.

The mall sits directly over Serangoon MRT/Bus Interchange, so a trip from Lorong Chuan MRT involves a single stop on the Circle Line with no additional walking beyond the station itself.

Nearby Green Spaces & Food

Parks & Serangoon Garden Hawker Fare

A couple of small neighbourhood parks sit within a few hundred metres of the Lorong Chuan area, and Serangoon Garden's hawker and cafe scene is a short distance away.

🌱
Chiltern Drive Interim Park
A small neighbourhood park in the Lorong Chuan area — a short walk for a quick green break.
🌲
Kampong Park @ Serangoon
Another nearby neighbourhood park.
🍴
Serangoon Garden
Long-established hawker centre and a cluster of cafes and restaurants, popular across the north-east for its local food scene.

For a larger regional park, Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park — one of central Singapore's biggest, with a 3km river and open lawns — is roughly a 10-minute drive away. Travel times from One Chuan Grove will be confirmed once the site's address is assigned.

FAQ

NEX & Serangoon — Frequently Asked Questions

How far is NEX mall from One Chuan Grove?
NEX sits above Serangoon MRT, one Circle Line stop from Lorong Chuan MRT. Walking time from One Chuan Grove to Lorong Chuan MRT will be confirmed once the site's address is assigned.
What's at NEX?
A supermarket, cinema, the Serangoon Public Library, and a wide mix of retail and dining, plus direct access to the Serangoon Bus Interchange.
Are there parks near the site?
Two small neighbourhood parks — Chiltern Drive Interim Park and Kampong Park @ Serangoon — are nearby. Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park, a much larger regional park, is about a 10-minute drive away.
