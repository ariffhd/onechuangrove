Project Core

One Chuan Grove — Development Overview & Project Factsheet

One Chuan Grove is a forthcoming mixed development at Lorong Chuan, District 19 — 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space, per URA Developer's Licence C1558. The project is developed by Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd., the project vehicle of a joint venture between Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. (65%) and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. (35%), which was awarded the site through two Government Land Sales tenders in 2025.

The unit mix, facilities and architectural design will be announced closer to launch.

The Developer Partnership

Sing Holdings & Sunway Developments

One Chuan Grove is developed by a two-way joint venture pairing an established SGX-listed Singapore developer with the Singapore property arm of a major Malaysian group — the partnership awarded both Government Land Sales parcels at Lorong Chuan.

Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. (65%)
A subsidiary of Sing Holdings Limited, SGX Mainboard-listed since 2007 and active in Singapore property since 1964. Past residential projects include North Gaia (Yishun EC), Parc Botannia (Sengkang, with Wee Hur Holdings), Robin Residences and The Laurels — winner of the FIABCI Singapore Property Award 2015.
Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. (35%)
A wholly-owned subsidiary of Malaysia-listed Sunway Berhad, and the entity behind Sunway Group's Singapore real estate arm — rebranded Sunway MCL following its 2025 acquisition of MCL Land. Close to 20 years active in the Singapore market, with a portfolio including Tembusu Grand, The Continuum and Parc Esta.

Site & Masterplan

Two Amalgamated GLS Parcels at Lorong Chuan

One Chuan Grove is formed from two adjoining Government Land Sales parcels at Lorong Chuan, District 19, both won by the Sing Holdings-Sunway joint venture in 2025 and amalgamated under Lots 19037L and 19064A MK18. Per URA Developer's Licence C1558, the combined site provides 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space.

The tower configuration, facilities and architectural design will be announced closer to launch.

Connectivity & Transit

Connectivity at Lorong Chuan

One Chuan Grove is located near Lorong Chuan MRT Station (CC14, Circle Line) — one stop from Bishan (North-South Line interchange) and one stop from Serangoon (North East Line interchange), with the now-complete Circle Line loop reaching Marina Bay and HarbourFront without a transfer.

About the Site

Two Parcels, One Development

One Chuan Grove is being developed on two adjoining Government Land Sales (GLS) parcels at Lorong Chuan.

The first parcel, released under the 2H2024 GLS Programme, was awarded to our joint venture — Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. — when its tender closed on 8 July 2025. The adjoining 1H2025 parcel followed when its tender closed on 4 September 2025, allowing the two parcels to be developed as a single site.

On 31 July 2026, URA issued Housing Developer's Licence C1558 to Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd., the project company, for 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space across both lots.
