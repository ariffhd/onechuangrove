Project Core

One Chuan Grove — Development Overview & Project Factsheet

One Chuan Grove is a forthcoming mixed development at Lorong Chuan, District 19 — 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space, per URA Developer's Licence C1558. The project is developed by Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd., the project vehicle of a joint venture between Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. (65%) and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. (35%), which together committed $1.33 billion across two Government Land Sales tenders in 2025 to secure and amalgamate the site.

The combined development is reported to comprise five blocks of up to 27 storeys across the two amalgamated GLS parcels. The official unit mix, facility plans and architectural theme have not yet been released — check back as details are confirmed.

The Developer Partnership

Sing Holdings & Sunway Developments

One Chuan Grove is developed by a two-way joint venture pairing an established SGX-listed Singapore developer with the Singapore property arm of a major Malaysian group — the same partnership that topped both Chuan Grove GLS tenders.

Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. (65%)
A subsidiary of Sing Holdings Limited, SGX Mainboard-listed since 2007 and active in Singapore property since 1964. Past residential projects include North Gaia (Yishun EC), Parc Botannia (Sengkang, with Wee Hur Holdings), Robin Residences and The Laurels — winner of the FIABCI Singapore Property Award 2015.
Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. (35%)
A wholly-owned subsidiary of Malaysia-listed Sunway Berhad, and the entity behind Sunway Group's Singapore real estate arm — rebranded Sunway MCL following its 2025 acquisition of MCL Land. Close to 20 years active in the Singapore market, with a portfolio including Tembusu Grand, The Continuum and Parc Esta.

Architectural Vision

Site Elevation — A 5-Hectare Masterplan

One Chuan Grove occupies one of the largest single residential plots available for private development in District 20 — approximately 540,000 sq ft (5 hectares). This scale enables meaningful separation between towers and the kind of landscaped communal facilities that define a true residential estate.

The site's elevated terrain along Bright Hill Drive contributes to a natural stack differentiation, with higher floors commanding unobstructed views across the Central Catchment Nature Reserve and MacRitchie Reservoir. One Chuan Grove comprises 6 towers — 2 blocks of 30 storeys (Luxury Collection) and 4 blocks of 21 storeys (Classic Collection) — with the Luxury Collection expected to enjoy fantastic views, including towards the SICC side.

North-facing and reservoir-facing stacks are expected to command a facing premium at launch — a pattern consistent with Jadescape (2018), the closest large-scale RCR precedent for this site.

Connectivity & Transit

Unmatched Connectivity at Bright Hill Drive

One Chuan Grove's most structurally compelling attribute — one that directly underpins both liveability and long-term investment value — is its access to three distinct MRT lines within a short walking radius. This is precisely why UOL, SingLand and CapitaLand were willing to pay $810 million for this site.

TEL · Thomson-East Coast Line
Doorstep to Upper Thomson MRT (TE8)
Upper Thomson MRT connects residents directly to the CBD (Marina Bay, ~22 mins), Orchard Road (~14 mins) and Gardens by the Bay — all without a transfer. Walk from One Chuan Grove estimated at 5–8 minutes.
Walk-to-MRT specification materially affects rental yield and resale capital appreciation.
CRL · Cross Island Line (2030)
The Bright Hill Interchange — 2030 Connectivity Multiplier
Bright Hill Station will become a full TEL × CRL interchange in 2030 — Singapore's eighth MRT line. Direct access to Ang Mo Kio, Jurong Lake District (second CBD) and Changi Airport without transfer.
Properties at upcoming MRT interchanges have historically appreciated ahead of opening. Buyers entering at 2026 launch pricing position for this structural premium.
NSC · North-South Corridor (2027)
North-South Corridor — Reducing CBD Drive Time
Singapore's longest transit priority corridor (21.5 km) opens from 2027, running Woodlands–CBD via Ang Mo Kio and Bishan. LTA estimates suggest ~15-minute reduction in peak-hour CBD drive time from Upper Thomson.
A structural valuation tailwind not yet fully priced into the District 20 market.

Background

The GLS Story — How One Chuan Grove Came to Be

One Chuan Grove rises from two adjoining Government Land Sales (GLS) parcels at Lorong Chuan — both won by the same joint venture within nine weeks of each other, an unusually decisive back-to-back result even by GLS standards.

The first parcel, released under the 2H2024 GLS Programme, drew seven bids when its tender closed on 8 July 2025. A joint venture between Sing Holdings Residential Pte. Ltd. and Sunway Developments Pte. Ltd. topped the field at $703.6 million — $1,376 per square foot per plot ratio (psf ppr) — 7.3% above the next-highest bid of $1,282 psf ppr from City Developments. It was the second-highest psf ppr ever recorded for a suburban private GLS plot, and the first private residential GLS site awarded in the Lorong Chuan area since 2009.

Two months later, the same consortium returned for the adjoining 1H2025 GLS parcel. Its tender closed on 4 September 2025 with five bids; the JV's $623.9 million offer — $1,331 psf ppr — beat the next-highest bid of $606.06 million ($1,293 psf ppr) from COLI (Singapore) by 2.9%. Securing both parcels let the JV amalgamate them into a single development. On 31 July 2026, URA issued Housing Developer's Licence C1558 to Chuan Grove Pte. Ltd. — the consortium's project vehicle — for 1,056 units of flats/apartments with commercial space across both lots, clearing the way for One Chuan Grove's official sales launch.
